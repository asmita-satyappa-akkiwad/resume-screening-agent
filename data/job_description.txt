Job Title: Junior AI Research Associate

About the role:
We are looking for a Junior AI Research Associate to join our team.
The ideal candidate has hands-on experience building and evaluating
machine learning and NLP systems, and is comfortable working with
Python in a fast-paced research environment.

Responsibilities:
- Build and evaluate NLP and machine learning models
- Work with APIs and backend services to deploy models
- Collaborate with senior researchers on experiments
- Write clean, well-documented Python code

Required Skills:
- Strong proficiency in Python
- Experience with machine learning and NLP (e.g. scikit-learn, pytorch, or tensorflow)
- Familiarity with REST APIs
- Experience with SQL or another database technology
- Git version control

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with Docker
- Experience with AWS or another cloud platform
- Familiarity with Flask or Django
- Experience with data visualization tools

Education:
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.

Experience:
1+ years of relevant experience (internships and strong academic
projects are acceptable).
